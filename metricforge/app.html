Customer Churn Rate

Churn = Customers lost ÷ Customers at start × 100

–

Benchmark (SMB SaaS, monthly): <5% excellent · 5–10% typical · >10% serious retention problem. Net growth this period: –

Revenue Churn & Net Revenue Retention

Revenue churn = MRR lost ÷ MRR at start × 100  ·  NRR = (Start + Expansion − Churned − Contraction) ÷ Start × 100

–

NRR benchmark: ≥110% excellent · 100–110% good · <100% shrinking base. NRR: –

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

LTV = (ARPA × Gross margin %) ÷ Monthly churn rate

–

Implied customer lifetime: – months. Uses the contribution-margin method (correct for unit economics).

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

CAC = Total sales & marketing spend ÷ New customers acquired

–

Fully loaded = salaries, tools, ad spend, content, events — not just ads.

LTV : CAC Ratio

LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CAC — uses your LTV and CAC inputs above

–

Benchmark: ≥3 healthy (invest more) · 1–3 fix before scaling · <1 losing money on every customer.

CAC Payback Period

Payback (months) = CAC ÷ (ARPA × Gross margin %)

–

Benchmark: ≤12 mo good · 12–18 acceptable for higher ACV · >24 dangerous. Uses ARPA, margin, CAC from your inputs above.

Quick Ratio

Quick Ratio = (New MRR + Expansion MRR) ÷ (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR)

–

Benchmark: ≥4 excellent · 2–4 healthy · <2 growth is being eaten by churn · <1 shrinking.

Magic Number

Magic Number = Net New ARR (this quarter) ÷ S&M spend (prior quarter)

–

Benchmark: ≥1.0 efficient growth (invest more) · 0.5–1.0 moderate · <0.5 inefficient — fix GTM before spending.

Rule of 40

Rule of 40 = Revenue growth % + Profit margin %

–

Benchmark: ≥40% healthy SaaS · 0–40% acceptable at scale · <0% unsustainable.

Runway

Runway (months) = Cash on hand ÷ Net monthly burn

–

Benchmark: ≥18 mo comfortable · 12–18 fine · <12 raise or cut · <6 critical. Out-of-money date: –
